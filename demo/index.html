Xiaomi MiMo evaluation project

Agentic civic content, with sources before slogans.

A multi-agent workflow for Indonesian creators: trend scouting, research, policy analysis, content generation, and safety review before manual publishing.

5M-15M

target daily tokens for long-chain research, generation, review, and coding iterations.

01

Trend Scout

Collects topics from trend mirrors, RSS, and user prompts.

02

Research

Extracts claims, numbers, dates, source links, and uncertainty.

03

Policy Analyst

Turns facts into civic context and public-interest questions.

04

Content Studio

Creates X threads, short video scripts, captions, and Telegram briefs.

05

Safety Review

Flags unsupported allegations, SARA risk, and excessive certainty.

Input: "Rupiah exchange rate is trending"

Output:
- verified claim checklist
- public impact angle
- X thread draft
- 30s video script
- safety notes
Responsible-use rule:

The system drafts content only.
A human verifies sources and posts manually.
